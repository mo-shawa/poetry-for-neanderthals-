# It Dog privacy policy

It Dog doesn't collect any information about you. There are no accounts, ads,
analytics or trackers, and the app doesn't send anything over the network.

Team names, player names, settings, the game in progress and the list of cards
you've already seen are saved only on your device (in the browser's local
storage, or the app's storage on iOS and Android) so the next game can pick up
where you left off. Clearing the site's data, or deleting the app, removes them.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here.
